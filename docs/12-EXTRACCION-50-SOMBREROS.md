## PY hats (16-30) — summary of decisions

- **CONATEL (16)**: Bluetooth = telecom equipment — homologation label check at supplier onboarding (CDE wholesaler's paperwork; ours only if direct-1688). Non-RF SKUs (cases, wired chargers, cables) = import-first candidates.
- **Cash sweep (17)**: daily messenger-float reconciliation; supplier terms renegotiated at 30 clean orders.
- **Zones (18)****: free-shipping-over-X steering + pickup points for far zones.
- **Anti-counterfeit (19)**: serial check at inbound; one-strike supplier delist; never sell unverifiable brands.
- **Fleet (20)**: per-delivery→daily-rate crossover at ~15/day; evening (18-21h) staffing first.
- **Copy (21)**: voseo PY register; price anchors written out in guaraní words; weekly script A/B via eval harness.
- **Bank recon (22)**: QR reference auto-match; transfer fuzzy-match; DNIT retention file monthly.
- **Importer (23)**: courier consignment until ~Gs. 40M/mo; then RUC importador + HS pre-classification.
- **Social proof (24)**: photo-review incentive; Google Business Profile worked weekly.
- **Tax calendar (25)**: RESIMPLE monthly + F.502 + patente renewal + 80%-cap alert automated.
- **Warranty reserve (26)**: failure-rate-funded reserve; 2-3% returns budget; DOA>5% auto-pause.
- **WhatsApp list (27)**: opt-in broadcast list (7593-compliant); niche-segmented; weekly max.
- **Supplier scorecard (28)**: price/fill/delivery/failure/warranty scoring; quarterly review; 5-SKU trial protocol.
- **Flash sales (29)**: fechas playbook rehearsed; 48h sell-out cap; post-flash data → buy list.
- **Expansion (30)**: gate = 150 sales/mo AND ≥12% net margin × 3 months; FB-group demand probe first.
